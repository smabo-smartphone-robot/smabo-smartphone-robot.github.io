Roadmap#

This page is the guide for the 'Hand' node on the roadmap below.

This guide assumes you have completed the smabo-esp32 setup.

Overview#

This page explains the Hand feature.

Specifically, you will implement:

  • Manual controller control of the hand

  • Random motion control of the hand

Assembly#

Assemble the required parts as shown in the video below.

Assembly tips

The assembly video does not show how to attach the SG90 servos, so the steps are described here.


First, fix the SG90 between sg90_plate_1 and sg90_plate_2 as shown, then attach the assembly to the body.


Slide sg90_base over from the top and fit the horn in. No tools required, but you may optionally screw the horn to the servo using the included screws.

Servo Settings#

Servo settings can be changed in the 'Modes/servos' section of the 'Config' tab in smabo-web.

First, since the left and right hand servos are mounted in opposite directions, enable:

  • 'inverse' for 'right_hand' only


Next, enable:

  • 'left_hand' and 'right_hand'

Manual Control#

Steps for manual controller control.

In the 'Config' tab → 'Modes/servos', for left_hand and right_hand, set:

  • 'mode' to 'manual'


Adjust servo angles in the 'Servo' tab to move both arms.

Random Control#

Steps for random motion control.

In the 'Config' tab → 'Modes/servos', for left_hand and right_hand, set:

  • 'mode' to 'random'
  • 'group' to 'hands'

random_group

Servos assigned to the same random_group will move at the same random time.


If you want the left and right hands to move at independent random times, assign left_hand and right_hand to different random_groups.


New random_groups can be added by clicking the "Add random Group" button.


After a moment, both hands will move at random intervals.
